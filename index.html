How it works

Four steps, and one of them is yours.

There is no survey visit, no deposit and no contract. You send a photo, we send a price, and if it suits you we come.

1

Send a photo, get a price

Message us the item on WhatsApp — a picture of the sofa, the size of the rug, the model of the car. A price and a time slot come back to you before anybody is booked in.

2

We arrive with everything

The steam extractor, the water, the power leads and covers for your floors all come with us. Nothing is taken away to a workshop and nothing is borrowed from your kitchen.

3

Pre-treat, steam, extract

Stains are treated first. Then hot steam goes deep into the fabric and the extractor draws the dirt and the water back out in the same pass — which is the part that makes it dry quickly.

4

Dry, and back in use

We groom the pile, air the room and tell you honestly how long to leave it before sitting down. Most upholstery is comfortable again the same day.

Questions

The things people ask before booking.

How long does it take to dry?

Upholstery is usually touch-dry in two to four hours and fully dry the same day. Thick carpet takes longer in a humid month. The extractor pulls most of the water back out as it goes, which is what makes the difference — and the crew will give you a real answer on the day rather than a guess.

Is it safe for children and pets?

Yes. The products are non-toxic and most of the cleaning is done by heat and water rather than solvents. There is no chemical smell afterwards and no reason to keep anyone out of the room once the surface is dry to the touch.

Are you really open at three in the morning?

The listing says open 24 hours and we mean it. Emergencies with fabric are almost always spills, and a spill dealt with tonight is a far smaller job than the same spill left until the morning.

Do I have to take the sofa or the rug anywhere?

No. Everything is done in your home, your office or your parking space. We arrive with the machines, the water and the covers for your floors, and we leave with all of it again.

What does it cost?

It depends on the number of seats, the size of the rug or the size of the car, and on the state of the fabric when we get there. Send a photo on WhatsApp and a price comes back before anybody is booked in. New customers get up to 20% off the first clean.

Which fabrics can you clean?

Cotton, linen, velvet, microfibre, leather and most synthetic blends. A few delicate materials need a low-moisture method instead of steam — send a photo and we will tell you which one your furniture needs before we come out.
